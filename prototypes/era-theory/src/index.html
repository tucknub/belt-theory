Reusable research system

We do not rank eras from records alone.

01

Define the eras

Leadership control, season cutoffs and inherited conditions are documented before scoring.

02

Build the evidence base

Seasons, personnel, transactions, coaching, injuries and context live in an auditable workbook.

03

Score multiple dimensions

Results matter most, but process, stability, development and resilience explain how those results happened.

04

Editorial principles

Evidence first. Atmosphere second.

The visual experience can be cinematic. The history cannot be fabricated.

Authentic people and events

Real athletes, executives, crowds, venues and historical moments require authentic editorial or archival imagery.

AI for design, not impersonation

Generated textures, maps, diagrams, lighting and abstract atmosphere are useful. Synthetic documentary history is not.

Visible uncertainty

Active seasons, immature draft classes and unresolved trades remain marked rather than forced into definitive scores.

Open methodology

Weights, formulas, sources and scenario tests remain visible so readers can challenge the conclusion intelligently.

What Era Theory becomes

A library of serious sports-era investigations.

Not a news site. Not a generic ranking engine. Each report earns its place by becoming a complete, updateable research project.
