01 / The premise

WE DO NOT JUST LIST
WHO HELD THE TITLES.

We measure what promotions actually built with them.

Belt Theory turns championship history into evidence about prestige, development, durability, concentration, opportunity and organizational decision-making.

The publication uses authentic archival and editorial photography whenever it depicts real people or real wrestling history. Abstract AI-assisted design may support the atmosphere, but it never impersonates the people or events being studied.
